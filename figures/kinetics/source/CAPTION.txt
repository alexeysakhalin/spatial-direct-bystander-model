Growth and background-loss checks of the configured model.

Living-target counts are divided by the initial 1,200 targets. Columns show the 2D, 3D and local-entry representations; rows show growth challenge A (A-C), growth challenge B (D-F), and the effector background-loss challenge (G-I). Each alternative is one 96 h realization at target-to-effector ratio 2:1 and seed 0. The teal line is the alternative; the dashed dark line is its paired baseline seed 0. Thin grey lines show baseline seeds 1 and 2, and shading spans the pointwise minimum and maximum of all three baseline realizations. This envelope is descriptive variation on fixed initial coordinate scaffolds, not a confidence interval or replicated uncertainty for the alternative.

Growth A and B use the previously specified growth-scale challenges derived from net reference rates of 0.72 and 1.07 per day in distinct source systems. Those rates set conditional low-pressure scales; they are not measured rates of the simulated target population. The background-loss challenge uses a reported bound from a different system and intentionally changes the reference balance of unstimulated effector division and death. All other prescribed model inputs are retained. Exact configurations and seeds accompany the raw data.

The six growth alternatives lie above their baseline N3 envelopes at every positive saved hour. Their interval and endpoint changes differ: the 2D growth B endpoint change is small relative to its earlier trajectory change. The background-loss alternatives have small target integral changes of mixed sign despite much larger effector populations. Every pairwise representation contrast crosses during the full trajectory. The absence of primary sampled-time flags for 2D growth A does not imply an unchanged curve; its largest paired target difference occurs at 6 h.

These are finite-configuration one-factor checks. They do not establish kinetic calibration, stochastic robustness, numerical convergence, or a pure effect of dimension. Baseline mesh and time-step dependence remains unresolved as a convergence claim. The local-entry scenario does not validate vascular transport, extravasation, or in vivo tissue. ECM and Tregs are absent, and CD4/CD8 agents share the assigned laws. IFNG and TNF have identical dimensionless fields with different assigned roles. Spatial occupancy, contacts and saved links are separate model observables, not measured contributions to cell death. Full field, antigen-composition and spatial results accompany the trajectory figure.
