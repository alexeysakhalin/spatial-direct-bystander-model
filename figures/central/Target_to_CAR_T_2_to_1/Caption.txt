Supplementary spatial model of direct and cytokine-associated bystander responses at an initial target:CAR-T ratio of 2:1.

Simulations start with 1,200 targets (840 Ag+ and 360 Ag-) and 600 CAR-T cells. A, 2D monolayer in closed medium. B, 3D spheroid in closed medium. C, local-entry tissue scenario with external bath exchange of ten total medium volumes over 96 h. Snapshots show the preselected seed 0 at 0, 24 and 96 h. The 3D view omits the x>0, y<0 quarter; the local-entry view displays |z| <= 60 micrometres. Scale bars: A and C, 200 micrometres; B, 100 micrometres. Blue, orange, green and grey indicate living Ag+ targets, living Ag- targets, living CAR-T cells and retained dead cells, respectively.

Graphs show whole-domain living-cell counts from 97 hourly states for each of three stochastic realizations (seeds 0, 1 and 2). Thin lines denote individual realizations, thick lines their mean, and shading the pointwise minimum-to-maximum range, not a confidence interval. Initial coordinates are fixed; stochastic events and antigen-label placement vary. Graphs include cells omitted from the displayed section.

The model combines assigned contact and soluble-signal response rules. IFN-gamma and TNF have identical dimensionless central fields with different assigned roles; the TNF-associated response does not identify necroptosis or measured pathway contributions. Cross-panel comparisons combine spatial and medium conditions and do not isolate dimensionality. Local entry has no explicit vessels, ECM or Tregs, and CD4/CD8 labels share the same laws. Material grid and time-step dependence remains; the detailed methods report the numerical checks and parameter assumptions. These results describe the stated computational scenarios and do not establish quantitative experimental calibration.
