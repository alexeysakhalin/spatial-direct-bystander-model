Snapshots show the preselected seed 0 realization at 0, 24 and 96 h. They are not mean cellular configurations or a seed selected for its outcome. Graphs show whole-domain counts from 97 hourly saved states in each of three stochastic realizations (seeds 0, 1 and 2). Thin lines are individual runs, thick lines the arithmetic mean, and shaded areas the pointwise min-max range. These descriptive ranges are not confidence or population prediction intervals. Simulation events and target antigen-label placement vary on a fixed coordinate scaffold; initial geometries are not independently sampled.

Targets initially comprise 840 Ag+ and 360 Ag- cells, a rounded external U373/HER2 antigen example (Hegde et al., 2013, doi:10.1038/mt.2013.185). Universal cytokine-response competence is an assumption. IFN-gamma suppresses target-cycle entry; prior IFN-gamma priming enables a delayed TNF-associated model response. IFN-gamma/TNF concentrations use relative units, with zero intrinsic removal as an explicit retention limit, not a measured protein lifetime. Total modeled domain-plus-bulk volume is 3.456 microlitres. The 3D scenarios start CAR-T in a 200-240 micrometre radial shell around an approximately 180 micrometre target aggregate.

Panel C has no explicit vessels, ECM or resident immune cells; infiltration kinetics are not physiologically calibrated. CD4 and CD8 labels share model laws, Tregs are absent, and the absorbing exhausted transition is disabled. Cross-panel differences involve multiple conditions and cannot isolate pure dimensionality. Counts do not identify measured death-mechanism fractions. The contrasts are conditional on the stated discretization and model assumptions; repeat simulations alone do not establish numerical or biological adequacy.

Graphs report absolute living-cell counts. The companion analyses also retain all hourly target counts normalized by 1,200 initial targets, interval integrals, residual antigen composition and spatial readouts. IFN-gamma and TNF have identical dimensionless fields in this central configuration and different assigned roles; their separate labels do not identify independent biological contributions. Matched medium-exchange controls vary bath flow within a representation and are distinct from the combined cross-panel comparison. Those controls and the prescribed 2:1 kinetic alternatives each use one seed per altered condition; complete trajectories include crossings and small or reversed differences.

Step and grid challenges change target trajectories, residual populations, exposure fields and spatial access. In local entry, the central 96 h residual range is 147–164 cells; it is 284–306 on the refined grid and 232–279 after additionally halving the fine-grid diffusion step. The latter refinement does not recover the central residual population. General numerical convergence and quantitative biological calibration are not established.

The assigned TNF-associated response is not a molecular model or an experimental identification of necroptosis. The supplementary simulation does not estimate RIPK1/RIPK3/MLKL pathway activity or measured death-mechanism fractions.
