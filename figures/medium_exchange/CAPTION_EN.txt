Paired external-medium exchange comparisons for targets:CAR-T = 2:1 (1200 targets, 600 effectors initially). Columns show 2D culture, a 3D spheroid, and the tissue-entry scenario. Rows show living target counts, living antigen-negative target counts, and spatial-domain volume-weighted mean cytokine concentration. Closed medium is compared with the prescribed external exchange of ten combined domain-plus-reservoir volumes over 96 hours. Within each column the input difference is external reservoir flow only; cell positions, labels, rules, seed and all other numerical settings are identical. Total medium volume is 3.456 microlitres. This is a conditional model comparison, not a measured vascular-flow effect.

Curves use all 97 saved hourly states of each accepted simulation (seed 0, one realization per condition). No confidence band or statistical significance is estimated. Sharing a seed does not establish a matched stochastic error estimate. The three columns differ in multiple geometric and spatial conditions; their differences do not isolate dimensionality.

IFNg and TNF fields are numerically equal in these runs because the relevant source and transport settings are tied; the shared curve does not demonstrate biological equivalence. Concentrations are relative model units. Intrinsic IFNg/TNF loss and uptake are zero in this retention-limit reference. Cell counts combine division, direct contact-associated damage, the assigned cytokine-response law and background death; they are not measured fractions attributable to individual death mechanisms. Antigen-negative means the explicit Ag0 class, with zero assigned direct attack rate.

The transport and response parameters define a conditional model. Numerical refinements materially affect several predictions; general numerical convergence and quantitative biological calibration are not established. These paired comparisons use one realization per condition and do not establish robustness to stochastic variation. Parameter evidence and the completed sensitivity and numerical checks are included in the accompanying analyses.
